📋 Game Rules

Simple rules for maximum fun!

1

👥 Two Players Only

Each team must have exactly 2 members. One Navigator (blindfolded) and one Guide (sighted). Choose your partner wisely!

2

🙈 Complete Blindfold

Navigator must wear the blindfold for ALL 3 rounds. No peeking, no exceptions! Complete trust is the goal.

3

🗣️ Voice Only

Guide can ONLY use verbal instructions. No touching, pulling, or pushing the Navigator. Words are your only tool!

4

⏱️ Beat The Clock

Each round has a time limit. Complete all checkpoints before time runs out. Remember: penalties add to your time!

5

⚠️ Avoid Penalties

Every obstacle hit = -5 seconds penalty! Navigate carefully to minimize your final time and maximize your score.

6

🏃 Judge's Decision Final

All decisions made by event judges are final. Play fair, have fun, and respect the rules of the game!
